# Emergency Procedure

## Service unavailable

1. Check internet access and the production URL from a second device.
2. Switch to the agreed manual order/reservation log; do not fabricate completed payments in the system later.
3. Record time, affected module, user role, and screenshots.
4. Contact the designated technical support contact.

## Payment uncertainty

1. Search by order number before retrying.
2. Check whether the order is already paid and whether a payment receipt/reference exists.
3. Preserve payment evidence and escalate rather than creating a duplicate transaction.

## Account issue

1. Do not share passwords or use another staff member's account.
2. Ask an owner/admin to disable or reset access through the approved Supabase/Auth procedure.

## Recovery

Only the designated technical owner may run database migrations, restore data, or alter production policies.
