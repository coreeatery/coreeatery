# User Guide Cashier

## Start of shift

1. Sign in with your cashier account.
2. Open the register and create/open your shift with the actual opening cash.
3. Confirm the menu and table list are current before taking orders.

## Create an order

1. Start a new order and add at least one available menu item.
2. Verify quantity, table/customer, and totals before continuing.
3. Move the order through its operational status only when the kitchen/service state actually changes.

## Take payment

1. Open the correct unpaid order.
2. Confirm the total with the customer.
3. Enter payment method and amount once, then wait for the confirmation result.
4. Never retry a payment merely because the screen is slow; first refresh/search the order and confirm whether it is already paid.

## End of shift

1. Reconcile physical cash with the register.
2. Close the shift with the actual cash amount.
3. Report a mismatch immediately; do not edit historical payments to force a match.
